ingredients:
  - 1 pound rigatoni or penne
  - 1 tablespoon olive oil
  - 1 pound Italian sausage, casings removed
  - 1 small onion, diced
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional
  - 1 14.5-ounce can diced tomatoes
  - 1 cup tomato sauce
  - ½ cup heavy cream
  - ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  - 3 cups baby spinach
  - Salt and pepper
  - Fresh basil, for serving
instructions:
  - step:
    title: Cook the Pasta
    description: Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  - step:
    title: Brown the Sausage
    description: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it up, until browned.
  - step:
    title: Cook the Aromatics
    description: Add the onion and cook until softened. Stir in the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook for 30 seconds.
  - step:
    title: Make the Sauce
    description: Add the diced tomatoes and tomato sauce. Simmer for 8-10 minutes, then stir in the cream and parmesan.
  - step:
    title: Toss
    description: Add the pasta and spinach. Toss until the spinach wilts and the pasta is coated, adding reserved pasta water as needed.
  - step:
    title: Serve
    description: Season with salt and pepper and serve with basil and extra parmesan.

# Sausage Pasta

## Weeknight Pasta with Depth

Sausage pasta is useful because the sausage brings seasoning, fat, and
savory depth quickly. A little tomato, pasta water, and cheese can turn it
into a sauce that feels like it cooked longer than it did.

## Tips and Variations

- Brown the sausage well before building the sauce.
- Save pasta water so the sauce can cling to the noodles.
- Add spinach, kale, mushrooms, peas, roasted peppers, or broccoli rabe.
- Use spicy sausage if you want heat.
- Serve with a green salad and extra parmesan.
