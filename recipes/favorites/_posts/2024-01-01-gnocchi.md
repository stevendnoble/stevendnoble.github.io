ingredients:
  - 2 lb russet potatoes
  - 1 large egg, beaten
  - 1½ tsp kosher salt
  - 1¼-1½ cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  - 2 cups marinara sauce
  - 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  - ¼ cup grated parmesan, plus more for serving
  - Fresh basil, torn
instructions:
  - step:
    title: Cook the potatoes
    description: Bake potatoes at 400°F for 50-60 minutes, until completely tender. Split them open and let steam escape for 5 minutes, then scoop out the flesh.
  - step:
    title: Rice and cool
    description: Pass the warm potato through a ricer or food mill onto a clean work surface. Spread it out and let it cool until barely warm.
  - step:
    title: Make the dough
    description: Sprinkle with salt, drizzle over the beaten egg, and add 1¼ cups flour. Gently fold and press until a soft dough forms, adding a little more flour only if it feels too sticky to handle.
  - step:
    title: Shape
    description: Divide the dough into 4 pieces. Roll each into a rope about ¾ inch thick, cut into bite-size pieces, and roll over a fork or gnocchi board if you want ridges.
  - step:
    title: Boil
    description: Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil. Cook gnocchi in batches until they float, then give them another 30 seconds before lifting out with a slotted spoon.
  - step:
    title: Sauce and serve
    description: Warm marinara with butter or olive oil in a skillet. Add gnocchi and toss gently until coated. Finish with parmesan and basil.

# Potato Gnocchi

## Tender Potato Dumplings

Homemade gnocchi should feel soft and light, not dense. Baking the potatoes
keeps them dry, which means you can use less flour and keep the dough tender.
Once the gnocchi are shaped, the meal can stay simple: marinara, butter or
olive oil, parmesan, and basil.

## Tips and Variations

- Handle the dough gently and add only enough flour to make it workable.
- Boil in batches so the water stays at a gentle simmer.
- For pan-crisped gnocchi, boil first, then sear in butter or olive oil until
  golden.
- Freeze shaped uncooked gnocchi on a sheet pan, then boil from frozen.
- Serve with marinara, pesto, sage brown butter, or a simple garlic cream
  sauce.
