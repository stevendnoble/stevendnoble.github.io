ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 4 scallions, sliced
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 20 ounces frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed very dry
  - 8 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
  - ½ cup ricotta cheese
  - 2 eggs, beaten
  - ¼ cup chopped dill
  - ¼ cup chopped parsley
  - 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - 12 sheets phyllo dough, thawed
  - 6 tablespoons butter, melted
instructions:
  - step:
    title: Make the Filling
    description: Heat the olive oil in a skillet. Cook the scallions and garlic for 1-2 minutes, then stir in the spinach and cook until any remaining moisture evaporates. Cool slightly.
  - step:
    title: Add Cheese and Herbs
    description: In a bowl, combine the spinach mixture, feta, ricotta, eggs, dill, parsley, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
  - step:
    title: Layer the Bottom
    description: Heat the oven to 375°F. Brush a 9x13-inch baking dish with butter. Layer 6 sheets of phyllo in the dish, brushing each with melted butter.
  - step:
    title: Fill and Top
    description: Spread the spinach filling evenly over the phyllo. Top with remaining phyllo sheets, brushing each with butter.
  - step:
    title: Score and Bake
    description: Score the top into squares with a sharp knife. Bake for 35-45 minutes, until golden and crisp. Cool slightly before serving.

# Spanakopita

## Crisp Phyllo, Bright Spinach Filling

Spanakopita is all about the contrast between flaky phyllo and a salty,
herby spinach-feta filling. The filling should be flavorful but not wet, or
the pastry will lose its crispness.

## Tips and Variations

- Squeeze the spinach very well before mixing the filling.
- Keep phyllo covered with a towel so it does not dry out while you work.
- Serve with Greek salad, lemony potatoes, grilled chicken, or soup.
- Add dill, mint, parsley, scallions, or a little lemon zest.
- Assemble ahead and bake just before serving.
