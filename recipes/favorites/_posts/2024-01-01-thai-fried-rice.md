instructions:
  - step:
    title: Marinate the Shrimp
    description: In a bowl, combine the shrimp with the light soy sauce, kosher salt, white pepper, neutral oil, baking soda, and cornstarch. Marinate for 15 minutes.
  - step:
    title: Make the Garlic-Chili Paste
    description: In a mortar and pestle, pound the garlic and Thai chili peppers into a paste. Set aside.
  - step:
    title: Mix the Sauce
    description: In a small bowl, stir together the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, fish sauce, palm sugar, white pepper, and MSG.
  - step:
    title: Cook the Shrimp
    description: Heat 3 tablespoons neutral oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Saute the shrimp for 2-3 minutes, or until cooked through. Remove the shrimp and set aside.
  - step:
    title: Fry the Aromatics
    description: In the same pan, fry the garlic-chili paste for 15 seconds until fragrant. Immediately add the shallot and onion and stir-fry for 1 minute.
  - step:
    title: Scramble the Eggs
    description: Push the aromatics to the side of the pan. Add the beaten eggs and scramble them, adding 1 tablespoon more oil if needed. Mix the eggs into the aromatics.
  - step:
    title: Fry the Rice
    description: Add the day-old rice and stir-fry over high heat, breaking up any clumps, until the grains are toasted and relatively dry. Add the premixed sauce and toss until evenly combined.
  - step:
    title: Finish with Shrimp and Basil
    description: Add the cooked shrimp and basil and stir-fry for another 1-2 minutes over high heat. Garnish with lime wedges and extra basil.

# Thai Fried Rice

## Fast, Fragrant, and Built for High Heat

Thai fried rice should move quickly. Cold rice, high heat, and a hot pan help
the grains stay separate instead of turning soft. Fish sauce, soy sauce,
garlic, egg, lime, and herbs make it savory and bright.

## Tips and Variations

- Use day-old rice if possible.
- Cook ingredients in stages so the pan stays hot.
- Add chicken, shrimp, pork, tofu, pineapple, or extra vegetables.
- Serve with cucumber, lime wedges, cilantro, and chili fish sauce.
- Prep everything before the rice goes in because the final cooking is fast.
