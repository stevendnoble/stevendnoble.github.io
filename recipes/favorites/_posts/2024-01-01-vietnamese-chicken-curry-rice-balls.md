instructions:
  - step:
    title: Warm the Stock
    description: Add the chicken stock to a pot and bring just to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer.
  - step:
    title: Start the Risotto
    description: In a separate pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the arborio rice and cook for about 2 minutes, stirring so the butter fully coats the grains.
  - step:
    title: Cook the Risotto
    description: Add 1 cup warm stock and stir frequently until mostly absorbed. Continue adding stock one ladle at a time, about ½ cup, letting each addition absorb before adding the next. Stir frequently until the rice is al dente and creamy.
  - step:
    title: Chill the Risotto
    description: Remove the risotto from the heat. Spread it onto a parchment-lined sheet about ½-inch thick. Refrigerate until fully cooled; overnight is best for easier shaping.
  - step:
    title: Cook the Chicken
    description: Season the chicken thighs lightly with salt and pepper. Cook in a pan over medium heat until fully cooked and lightly browned. Remove, cool slightly, then finely chop or shred.
  - step:
    title: Make the Filling
    description: In the same pan, saute the shallot and garlic until fragrant. Add the chopped chicken back in, then stir in 1-2 teaspoons fish sauce and black pepper. Remove from the heat and mix with the mashed potato or taro until cohesive. The mixture should be soft but hold together when pressed.
  - step:
    title: Form the Rice Balls
    description: Scoop a baseball-sized portion of chilled risotto. Flatten it in your hand, add filling to the center, then enclose and shape into a tight ball. Roll lightly in flour and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  - step:
    title: Bread the Rice Balls
    description: Coat each chilled ball in beaten egg, then roll in panko breadcrumbs until fully covered. Refrigerate again for 30 minutes.
  - step:
    title: Fry the Rice Balls
    description: Heat oil to 350°F. Fry the rice balls until deep golden brown and crispy, about 4-6 minutes. Drain on paper towels.
  - step:
    title: Start the Curry
    description: In a pot, saute the diced onion until soft. Add the curry paste and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Add the lemongrass and stir.
  - step:
    title: Simmer the Curry
    description: Add the carrots and potatoes, tossing to coat. Pour in the coconut milk and water or stock. Bring to a simmer, then add fish sauce and sugar. Simmer for 15-25 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the curry is slightly thickened.
  - step:
    title: Adjust the Curry
    description: Taste and adjust with more fish sauce for salt and umami, a pinch of sugar for balance, or a squeeze of lime at the end.
  - step:
    title: Serve
    description: Ladle curry into a bowl. Place a crispy rice ball on top, garnish with cilantro, and serve with lime wedges if using.

# Vietnamese Chicken Curry Rice Balls

## Ca Ri Ga, Reimagined

These rice balls take the flavors of Vietnamese chicken curry and turn them
into something crisp, snackable, and easy to share. The filling should taste
savory and aromatic before it is shaped, because the rice softens the flavor
once everything is together.

## Air Fryer or Oven

For the air fryer, spray the rice balls well with oil and cook at 375°F until
crisp and hot, turning as needed. For the oven, bake on a parchment-lined
sheet at high heat and flip once so both sides brown. The air fryer gives the
best crispness with the least oil.

## Tips and Variations

- Rotisserie chicken is an easy shortcut.
- Keep the rice mixture compact when shaping so the balls hold together.
- Serve with herbs, pickled vegetables, cucumber, lime, or chili sauce.
- Make smaller balls for appetizers or larger ones for a meal.
- Chill before cooking if they feel loose.
