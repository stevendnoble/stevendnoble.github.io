ingredients:
  - 1 lb phyllo dough, thawed according to package directions
  - 1½ cups walnuts
  - 1½ cups pistachios, plus extra chopped pistachios for garnish
  - ½ cup almonds, optional
  - ½ cup granulated sugar
  - 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp kosher salt
  - 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  - 1 cup honey
  - ¾ cup water
  - ¾ cup granulated sugar
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - 1 tsp orange zest, optional
  - 1 cinnamon stick, optional
instructions:
  - step:
    title: Prepare the nut filling
    description: Pulse walnuts, pistachios, and almonds if using in a food processor until finely chopped but not powdered. Stir with sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  - step:
    title: Make the syrup
    description: Combine honey, water, sugar, lemon juice, orange zest if using, and cinnamon stick if using in a saucepan. Simmer for 8-10 minutes, then remove from heat and cool slightly.
  - step:
    title: Set up the phyllo
    description: Heat oven to 350°F. Brush a 9x13-inch baking dish with melted butter. Unroll phyllo and cover it with a lightly damp towel while you work so it does not dry out.
  - step:
    title: Layer
    description: Add 8 sheets of phyllo to the dish, brushing each sheet with butter. Sprinkle over a thin layer of nut filling. Continue with 4 buttered phyllo sheets between each nut layer, finishing with 8 buttered sheets on top.
  - step:
    title: Cut before baking
    description: Use a sharp knife to cut the baklava into diamonds or squares, cutting almost all the way through the layers.
  - step:
    title: Bake
    description: Bake for 45-55 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp. If the top browns too quickly, tent loosely with foil.
  - step:
    title: Syrup and rest
    description: Pour the warm syrup evenly over the hot baklava as soon as it comes out of the oven. Sprinkle with extra pistachios and let rest at least 4 hours before serving.

# Baklava

## Phyllo Tips

Keep the phyllo covered while you work. It dries quickly, and dry phyllo cracks instead of layering neatly. A few tears are completely fine, especially in the middle layers, so do not worry about making every sheet perfect.

## Syrup Timing

Hot baklava plus warm or room-temperature syrup works well because the pastry stays crisp while absorbing the syrup. Letting it rest is important; the texture is much better after a few hours.

## Serving Ideas

Serve with strong coffee, black tea, mint tea, or a small scoop of vanilla ice cream. It is rich, so small diamond pieces are usually the perfect size.

## Variations

Use all walnuts for a classic, earthy version, all pistachios for something more luxurious, or add a little cardamom to the nut mixture. A splash of rose water or orange blossom water can go into the syrup, but keep it light.

## Make-Ahead and Storage

Baklava is an excellent make-ahead dessert. Store covered at room temperature for up to 5 days. Avoid refrigerating unless necessary, since the fridge can soften the crisp phyllo layers.
