Welcome to My Pokédex

Try searching for Pikachu or entering its Pokédex number, 25. The app fetches the matching Pokémon from PokéAPI and shows its sprite and types. Select Capture to add it to My Box, or Release to remove it. The linked tutorial walks through the search form, the API request, and the Handlebars templates that render both sets of cards.
