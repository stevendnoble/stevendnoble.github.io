ingredients:
  - 4 cups chicken stock
  - 2 stalks lemongrass, smashed and cut into pieces
  - 6 slices galangal or ginger
  - 4 makrut lime leaves, torn, optional
  - 1 pound boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  - 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 can coconut milk, 13.5 ounces
  - 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  - 1 teaspoon sugar
  - 2 tablespoons lime juice, plus more to taste
  - 1-2 Thai chilies, sliced, optional
  - Fresh cilantro, for garnish
  - Sliced scallions, for garnish
instructions:
  - step:
    title: Infuse the Broth
    description: Bring the chicken stock to a simmer with lemongrass, galangal, and makrut lime leaves if using. Simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  - step:
    title: Cook the Chicken
    description: Add the chicken and mushrooms and simmer until the chicken is cooked through, about 6-8 minutes.
  - step:
    title: Add Coconut Milk
    description: Stir in the coconut milk, fish sauce, sugar, and chilies if using. Heat gently without boiling hard.
  - step:
    title: Finish with Lime
    description: Remove from the heat and stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust with more fish sauce, lime, or sugar.
  - step:
    title: Serve
    description: Garnish with cilantro and scallions. The lemongrass, galangal, and lime leaves are for flavoring and are usually not eaten.

# Tom Kha Soup

## Creamy, Bright, and Aromatic

Tom Kha works because it is rich and bright at the same time. Coconut milk
gives the soup body, while lime, fish sauce, lemongrass, galangal, and lime
leaves keep it sharp and fragrant.

## Tips and Variations

- Simmer the aromatics long enough to perfume the broth, then strain if you
  want a smoother soup.
- Add chicken, shrimp, mushrooms, tofu, tomatoes, or extra vegetables.
- Finish with lime juice off the heat so it stays fresh.
- Serve with jasmine rice or Thai fresh rolls.
- Reheat gently so the coconut milk does not separate.
